Personal data privacy policy of Green Energy Environment Joint Stock Company Hung Yen (GMS) applicable to users submitting the online contact form.

A contact request that could not be emailed is held by the website for at most 7 days and is then deleted.

Technical recovery copies kept by Cloudflare expire automatically, and the oldest copy exists for at most 14 days from the moment the contact request is received.
