The complex is a chain of functional areas, each with one clear job:

- **Reception:** trucks are weighed and unload into a covered hall to limit odour and leachate.
- **Sorting line:** trommel screens and conveyors split waste into streams: organic, plastic, metal and combustible.
- **Composting area:** the organic stream is composted into humus for soil improvement.
- **VPD area:** plastic and combustible fractions go through pyrolysis to recover oil, syngas and char.
- **RDF pressing:** the remaining combustible fraction is pressed into pellets that replace fuel in industrial kilns.
- **Control room and lab:** operations are monitored and output quality is checked.

The shared goal: send less to landfill and turn waste into products that can still be used.
